# AAIC — Softlogic Life Insurance PLC — Research File

> **Sources:** Integrated Annual Report 2025 (484 pp, primary), Q1 2026 interim (31 Mar 2026), both read 14 Jul 2026.
> **Position:** none. Candidate from Layer 2 insurance-sector thesis.
> **One-line thesis:** A genuinely high-quality life insurer (39% ROE, 27% GWP growth, improving persistency) whose float mechanically reinvests into a 12%+ yield world — bought only at a price that discounts three real risks: the parent, the tax contingency, and SLFRS 17 opacity.

---

## 1. What the company actually is

Pure life insurer, #2 in the market by GWP, ~316.4 Mn shares. Acquired 100% of Allianz Life Lanka (Jul 2025, Rs. 1,426 Mn cash) — runs as a separate licensed subsidiary. Products: endowment (long-duration savings, the CSM engine), group life (short-cycle), single premium.

**Ownership — the first red flag, confirmed:** Softlogic Capital PLC 50.16%, Dalvik Inclusion 18.81%, Milford Ceylon 18.81% → **87.8% concentrated; public float 12.16%** (barely compliant under CSE Option 1). Price discovery is thin; the stock trades on a sliver.

## 2. The four decisive questions (the sector method)

### Q1 — Portfolio yield and maturity profile → **thesis confirmed and quantified**
- Group financial investments ~Rs. 60 Bn; **79% government securities** (regulatory min 30).
- Maturity buckets (AR p.420 via risk section): **Rs. 20.8 Bn (~35%) matures within 1 year**; 5.2 Bn in 1–3 yrs; 32.1 Bn beyond 3 yrs.
- Investment yield history: 18.4% (2023) → 21.4% (2024, inflated by falling-rate capital gains) → **14.9% (2025, rates bottomed)**. With the long bond now at 12.6% and a third of the book rolling within 12 months, the running yield recovers mechanically — ~Rs. 20.8 Bn repricing ~200bps higher ≈ Rs. 400+ Mn incremental annual investment income before any new float.
- **Evidence they're already doing it:** T-bond holdings jumped 29.7 → 38.1 Bn during Q1 2026 alone — management is extending duration into the high-yield window.
- Cost side, visible and tolerable: Q1'26 OCI debt-FVOCI loss −336 Mn (Group) from rising yields — MTM pain on existing paper, ~2.4% of equity.

### Q2 — Is the float growing? → **yes, fast, accelerating**
- GWP growth: 14.1% (2023) → 19.9% (2024) → 26.9% (2025) → **+30% y/y in Q1 2026** (Company 11.69 Bn vs 8.98 Bn).
- Q1'26 mix (Group): renewal 5.86 Bn (+28%), first-year 2.34 Bn (+26%), group life 2.83 Bn (+44%), single 1.30 Bn (+128%).
- Annualised new business premium 8.97 Bn (+38.8%). Insurance contract liabilities (the float) grew 2.9 Bn in one quarter.

### Q3 — Persistency / lapse (the inflation counterweight) → **improving, not deteriorating**
- Year 1: 86% (was 83) · Year 2: 66% (was 61) · Year 3: 54% (was 51).
- The feared inflation-lapse effect is **not in the data** through Q1 2026. Honest structural note: Y3 at 54% means nearly half of policies still die by year three — a leaky bucket industry-wide; growth must outrun it (currently does).

### Q4 — Capital (RBC/CAR) → **compliant, but the trend is the story**
- CAR 245% vs 120% minimum — but declining: 395 → 287 → 367 → 298 → **245**.
- Cause identified: bullet repayment of the USD 15 Mn Finnfund/Norfund Tier II subordinated loan in 2025 (reduced regulatory capital) + Allianz acquisition + growth strain.
- **The Q1'26 mystery, probably solved:** Rs. 4,734 Mn new "loans and borrowings" appeared from zero, cash jumped to 7.9 Bn. Most likely a replacement Tier II raise to rebuild CAR after the repayment. *Unverified — homework item #1.*

## 3. Profitability & underwriting quality
- ROE 39% · ROCE 54.5% · Combined ratio 91.3% (with maturities & surrenders) → **underwriting is profitable before a rupee of investment income**. Rare and valuable.
- Net claims ratio 51.9%, expense ratios falling three years running (total expense/NEP: 49.0 → 42.9 → 39.3).
- Q1'26: PBT 881 Mn (Company), PAT 624 Mn (+31% y/y), EPS 1.97 (Company) / 2.11 (Group).

## 4. Earnings quality — the caveats that discount the P/E
1. **Actuarial-transfer dependence:** Q1'26 profit rests on a Rs. 720 Mn surplus transfer from the non-par fund approved by the appointed actuary (vs 420 Mn Q1'25). Profit timing is substantially actuarial discretion, and heavily **Q4-loaded** (of 2025's ~4.0 Bn company profit, ~3.5 Bn came after Q1).
2. **2024's 21.4% investment yield** included falling-rate capital gains — unrepeatable; don't extrapolate.
3. **SLFRS 17 changes everything from 2026:** company is applying the CA Sri Lanka SoAT — Q1 and Q2 2026 interims remain on old SLFRS 4; full restatement lands later this year. CSM defers profit into future periods; **the trailing P/E of 5.2 is measured under a standard that is being retired.** Opening retained earnings will be adjusted. Impact assessment: AR pp. 451–461. Until restated numbers exist, every earnings multiple carries an asterisk.

## 5. Red flags register
- **Parent leakage channel exists:** the Company's *entire* listed-equity book (Rs. 556.4 Mn) = related-party investments in group entities — small (~1% of portfolio) but the channel is live. Parent investments 305.8 Mn additionally. Watch every interim's related-party note for growth in these lines.
- **Dividend upstreaming — so far disciplined:** 2025 interim dividend 1,424 Mn = payout only 30.4%. A stressed 50% parent hasn't (yet) stripped the subsidiary. Payout ratio rising above ~50% would be the tell.
- **Tax contingencies are material:** disputed assessments include Rs. 3,007 Mn (Y/A 2018/19–2021/22, determined in favour of CGIR, under TAC appeal), Rs. 643.5 Mn (2022/23), Rs. 662.9 Mn (2014/15–2017/18), plus VAT cases. Gross exposure ≳ Rs. 4 Bn against 14 Bn equity (~30%). Directors call likelihood "remote"; life-insurance surplus taxation is an industry-wide dispute, but the number belongs in the floor.
- **Restricted regulatory reserve:** 798 Mn (Company) one-off surplus locked in govt securities; distributable only with IRCSL approval.
- **Low float (12.16%)** — thin price discovery; irrelevant at my order size, relevant to how violently the price can move on nothing.

## 6. Valuation (Layer 4) — DRAFT, confirm price before any order
- 2025 Group EPS 14.8 → at year-end price 77.60: **P/E 5.2, earnings yield 19.1%** vs long-bond hurdle 12.6% — passes with ~6.5pt margin, *on SLFRS 4 earnings*.
- DPS 4.50 → yield ~5.8% at 77.60, payout 30% (well covered, room to grow).
- NAV/share 44.25 (Group) → P/B ~1.8. Expensive on book, cheap on earnings — the standard shape of a 39% ROE business. Book is the wrong anchor here; earnings power and dividend are the anchors.
- **Floor 70 (draft):** ≈ 4.7× 2025 EPS / 6.4% dividend yield / earnings yield >21% — a price at which even a one-third SLFRS 17 earnings restatement downward still clears the 12.6% hurdle, and the tax contingency is paid for. 
- **Second tranche level: 63** (floor −10%).
- **Ceiling 100–105 (draft):** ~7× SLFRS 4 EPS; earnings yield falls toward 14%, margin over hurdle thins to noise given restatement risk.
- Last known price: 81.40 (31 Mar 2026). **If current price sits between 70 and 105: watchlist, no order.** Update this line with today's quote before deciding anything.
