# AAIC — Softlogic Life Insurance PLC — Research File

> **Sources:** Integrated Annual Report 2025 (484 pp, primary, read 14 Jul 2026), Q1 2026 interim (31 Mar 2026, primary), **Interim Financial Statements for the period ended 30 June 2026 (H1 2026 + standalone Q2, primary, board-approved 10 Aug 2026, reviewed 10 Aug 2026)**.
> **Position:** none. Candidate from Layer 2 insurance-sector thesis.
> **One-line thesis:** A genuinely high-quality life insurer (39% ROE, 27% GWP growth, improving persistency) whose float mechanically reinvests into a 12%+ yield world — bought only at a price that discounts three real risks: the parent, the tax contingency, and SLFRS 17 opacity.
> **This version folds in the H1 2026 interim (§9).** Floor and ceiling are held unchanged — no trigger event met the bar to move them — but two things changed materially enough to log: the tax-contingency register grew (~4 Bn → ~5.15 Bn gross), and public float roughly doubled (12.16% → 27.54%), apparently via Dalvik Inclusion's stake leaving the top-20 register. Read together with the price move (81.40 → 93.70), the margin of safety at the current quote has narrowed since the file was opened.

---

## 1. What the company actually is

Pure life insurer, #2 in the market by GWP, ~316.4 Mn shares. Acquired 100% of Allianz Life Lanka (now **Softlogic Life Insurance Lanka Limited**, Jul 2025, Rs. 1,426 Mn cash) — runs as a separate licensed subsidiary; purchase price allocation confirmed in this interim (identifiable assets Rs. 11,190 Mn, liabilities assumed Rs. 10,171 Mn, Rs. 407 Mn intangible recognised). Products: endowment (long-duration savings, the CSM engine), group life (short-cycle), single premium.

**Ownership — the first red flag, now materially changed:** Softlogic Capital PLC 50.16%, Milford Ceylon (Pvt) Ltd 18.81% — **Dalvik Inclusion, previously co-holder of 18.81%, no longer appears in the Top 20 Shareholders list** as at 30 June 2026. Public shareholding jumped from **12.16% to 27.54%** in the same period (public shareholders 5,645 → 6,307; float-adjusted market cap Rs. 2.99 Bn → Rs. 8.16 Bn). The magnitude is close enough to Dalvik's former stake that this is the most likely explanation, though this file has not confirmed it via a CSE announcement (see homework). **Either way, concentrated ownership eased from 87.8% to ~72.5% non-public — a genuine reduction in the thin-float risk this file has carried since its first draft**, not yet fully resolved (72.5% is still high) but a real directional improvement worth pricing differently than a static risk.

**New this update: Softlogic Life signed an agreement in June 2026 to acquire 60% of Diamond Life Insurance Company Limited, a licensed Bangladeshi life insurer, for ~USD 1.9 Mn** — completed in July 2026 (a post-balance-sheet-date event, per Note 14). Trivial in size relative to the Group (≈Rs. 620 Mn against Rs. 14.6 Bn equity) but a genuine strategic signal: the company is now expanding internationally via bolt-on M&A, one year after the domestic Allianz Life acquisition. Not yet a valuation input — no financials for Diamond Life are in this interim — but a new integration-risk thread to track from here.

## 2. The four decisive questions

### Q1 — Portfolio yield & structure → **thesis holding; realised-gains unwind now visible in the data**
- Group financial investments Rs. 61.0 Bn (30-Jun-2026) vs Rs. 57.0 Bn (31-Dec-2025) — the book kept growing. Treasury bonds + bills + repo + sovereign bonds ≈ Rs. 45.5 Bn (~74.5% of the book) — broadly in line with the ~79% government-securities weighting cited from the FY2025 Annual Report, no material shift in composition.
- **Net realised gains collapsed: Company H1 2026 Rs. 104.1 Mn vs Rs. 871.7 Mn H1 2025 — down 88%.** This is exactly the "2024's capital-gains-inflated yield won't repeat" risk named in §4 of the original file, now showing up directly in the P&L rather than as a forward-looking caveat.
- **The important finding: PBT still grew despite losing ~Rs. 768 Mn of realised gains.** Company PBT rose 7.5% (Rs. 1,739.7 Mn → Rs. 1,869.4 Mn) and PAT rose 14.9% (Rs. 1,221.1 Mn → Rs. 1,403.7 Mn) YoY for H1, meaning core underwriting and investment income more than absorbed the fading capital-gains tailwind — the same shape of finding JINS's file made about its own Q1'26 ("core insurance result improved, FV marks didn't hurt"). A genuine quality-of-earnings positive, not just a headline number.

### Q2 — Float growth → **strong, but decelerating quarter-on-quarter within H1**
- GWP (Company): H1 2026 Rs. 24.76 Bn vs H1 2025 Rs. 18.73 Bn = **+32.2%**, continuing Q1's pace (Q1 alone was +30%).
- **But the quarterly breakdown shows deceleration, not a straight line:** Q1 2026 PAT grew 31% YoY; **Q2 2026 PAT grew only 4.9% YoY** (Rs. 779.7 Mn vs Rs. 743.5 Mn), and Q2 PBT actually fell 7.8% YoY (Rs. 988.5 Mn vs Rs. 1,071.6 Mn). The cause is fully explained by §1 above — Q2 2025 alone carried Rs. 856.2 Mn of the H1 2025 realised gain, which essentially did not repeat in Q2 2026 (Rs. 20.7 Mn). **Read the deceleration as a base-effect artefact of fading capital gains, not a growth slowdown** — GWP itself kept accelerating through both quarters.
- Premium mix (Group, H1 2026 vs H1 2025): first-year +23.6%, renewal +26.9%, group life +63.6% (Allianz Life consolidation effect), single premium +82.5%. Insurance contract liabilities (the float) grew **+15% in six months** (Group: Rs. 46.6 Bn → Rs. 53.6 Bn).

### Q3 — Persistency / lapse → **still not disclosed this quarter — standing open item**
No Y1/Y2/Y3 ladder appears anywhere in the H1 2026 interim, same gap as every prior document. Original figures (86/66/54, improving) remain the most recent data point available; unchanged, unconfirmed.

### Q4 — Capital (RBC/CAR) → **not disclosed in this interim — standing open item**
No CAR figure appears anywhere in the H1 2026 interim (unusual, since it's normally a standard insurer KPI). The last confirmed reading remains **245%** from the FY2025 Annual Report. This is now the single most consequential missing number in the file, because the new borrowing (below) is exactly the kind of event a CAR trend would contextualise.

## 3. Profitability & underwriting quality — H1 2026 in context
- **Company (like-for-like):** PBT Rs. 1,869.4 Mn (+7.5% YoY), PAT Rs. 1,403.7 Mn (+14.9% YoY), EPS Rs. 4.44 (H1) vs Rs. 3.86 (H1 2025).
- **Group (first H1 with Softlogic Life Lanka fully consolidated):** PBT Rs. 1,983.7 Mn, PAT Rs. 1,461.3 Mn, EPS Rs. 4.62 — modestly ahead of Company, confirming the subsidiary is a small net-accretive addition rather than a drag.
- **Surplus-transfer timing shifted between quarters, net roughly flat for H1** — a live illustration of the actuarial-discretion risk already named in the original file: Q1 2026 transfer was Rs. 560 Mn (vs Rs. 913 Mn Q1 2025, **down** 39%); Q2 2026 transfer was Rs. 720 Mn (vs Rs. 420 Mn Q2 2025, **up** 71%). H1 aggregate: Rs. 1,280 Mn vs Rs. 1,333 Mn (−4%, essentially flat). **This is a confirmation of the named risk, not a resolution of it** — the same discipline other files in this vault apply to a live-but-not-newly-material data point.
- Net finance income (Group) Rs. 3,211.3 Mn vs Company Rs. 2,690.5 Mn — most of the YoY growth in this line is the Allianz Life consolidation, not organic Company growth (Company H1 net finance income +2.3% YoY only).
- Net asset value per share: Group Rs. 46.11 (30-Jun-2026) vs Rs. 43.20 (31-Dec-2025); Company Rs. 46.14 vs Rs. 41.92 — a real ~7-10% NAV build in six months.

## 4. Earnings quality — the caveats that discount the P/E (unchanged, one reinforced)
1. **Actuarial-transfer dependence** — reinforced this quarter (§3): the transfer moved 39% down in Q1, then 71% up in Q2, net flat for H1. Profit timing remains substantially actuarial discretion.
2. **2024's 21.4% investment yield included falling-rate capital gains — unrepeatable, and now visibly unwinding** (§1): H1 2026 realised gains are 88% below H1 2025's level. This is the risk already flagged in the file's own earnings-quality caveats, now showing up as data rather than as a forward risk.
3. **SLFRS 17 changes everything from 2026, and the transition is still not finalised as of this interim.** The Company confirms it remains on the SoAT (Statement of Alternative Treatment), applying SLFRS 4 for the first two 2026 interim periods; the interim states plainly that qualitative/quantitative SLFRS 17 disclosures are being withheld this quarter **"due to the absence of sufficiently reliable and finalised results, as the implementation and validation of SLFRS 17 are still in progress."** Full restatement is still pending — the trailing multiple below still carries the same asterisk it did in July.

## 5. Red flags register — updated
- **Parent leakage channel — no new data this quarter.** No interim dividend was declared or paid in H1 2026 (the Statement of Changes in Equity shows the last dividend line as "Interim dividend – 2025," booked in H2 2025). Payout discipline neither confirmed nor broken this update — watch H2.
- **Tax contingencies are now larger, not smaller.** The interim's contingencies note (13.2) discloses nine numbered items; summing the disclosed rupee figures gives **gross exposure ≈ Rs. 5.15 Bn**, up from the ≈Rs. 4 Bn cited from the Annual Report. Some individual figures moved in both directions versus the original file's citations (e.g., one item is now stated at Rs. 2,645.93 Mn vs the ≈Rs. 3,007 Mn figure previously cited; another expanded to Rs. 1,740.6 Mn covering four years of assessments) — this reads as a more granular, updated disclosure rather than a like-for-like reconciliation, but the aggregate is unambiguously larger. Directors' "remote likelihood" language is unchanged. **This is the one item in this update that should, if anything, argue for holding the floor multiple where it is rather than loosening it.**
- **The Rs. 4.73 Bn borrowing mystery — partially resolved, not fully.** Confirmed real: cash flow statement shows "Proceeds from long term borrowing Rs. 4,719,928k" and the balance sheet now carries **"Loans and borrowings" of Rs. 5,172,327k at 30 June 2026 (from zero at 31 Dec 2025)**, booked entirely at the Company level (Group and Company figures match exactly). **What's still not disclosed: tenor, rate, subordination status, or regulatory purpose** (i.e., whether this is genuinely the Tier II capital-rebuild homework item #1 speculated on) — none of that appears in this interim's notes. Confirmed to exist and growing modestly quarter-on-quarter (Rs. 4.73 Bn → Rs. 5.17 Bn); still an open question, now a live balance-sheet fact rather than a one-quarter anomaly.
- **Public float — materially improved, not fully resolved.** 12.16% → 27.54% (§1). Real, but 72.5% concentrated ownership is still a lot; don't treat this as removing the risk, treat it as a genuine partial credit.
- **Persistency ladder and CAR — both still undisclosed** (§2, Q3/Q4). CAR's absence from a routine insurer interim is itself worth flagging as new: it wasn't in this document at all, where the annual report always carries it.

## 6. Valuation (Layer 4) — unchanged levels, narrower margin at the current price
- **TTM EPS (Group), rebuilt from FY2025 (14.8) − H1 2025 Company proxy (3.86) + H1 2026 Group (4.62) ≈ Rs. 15.56** — a modest improvement on FY2025's 14.8, but built on a base with the same actuarial-timing and capital-gains-unwind caveats as before. Not treated as a basis for revising sustainable EPS; per the standing rule, that requires a full-year confirmation or the SLFRS 17 restatement, not a six-month blend of Company and Group figures across an acquisition boundary.
- **Current price, confirmed via this interim's own Investor Information (§16.2): last traded Rs. 93.70 (30 June 2026)**, quarterly range Rs. 81.20–98.90 — up from the 31-Mar print of 81.40 cited in the original file. NAV/share (Group) Rs. 46.11 → P/B ≈ 2.03×, essentially unchanged from before.
- **At 93.70: trailing P/E ≈ 6.0× (on the TTM 15.56 estimate), earnings yield ≈ 16.6%** — still clears the 12.6% hurdle, but the margin has compressed from ~19.1% (at 77.60/14.8 in the original file) purely because price (+21%) outran earnings (+5%) over the period. This is exactly the kind of move the system exists to track, not react to.
- **Floor 70 / second tranche 63 / ceiling 100–105 — held unchanged.** No named risk was resolved cleanly enough, and no sustainable-EPS re-estimate is confirmed enough, to justify moving either number this update. The price (93.70) sits meaningfully closer to the ceiling than it did in July — worth naming plainly: **at last look this file was "watchlist, price between 70 and 105"; it is now "watchlist, price in the upper half of that band."** Still no mechanical signal either way.
