## A. The Business

Sri Lanka's largest conglomerate. **Seven reportable segments:**

| Segment | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Retail** | Keells supermarkets, logistics centre, office automation, **new-energy vehicle imports** |
| **Leisure** | City hotels, resorts (SL + Maldives), destination management — incl. Cinnamon Life |
| **Financial Services** | Union Assurance (90%), Nations Trust stake, leasing, broking |
| **Transportation** | Colombo port terminals (equity-accounted), bunkering, aviation services |
| **Consumer Foods** | Elephant House beverages, ice cream, processed foods (via CCS) |
| **Property** | Development + commercial office rental |
| Others | Plantations, IT, holding company |

### Segment split — FY2025/26 (revenue Rs 538.4Bn gross / 528.9Bn external)

| Segment | Revenue | % | PBT | PBT FY25 | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Retail** | 314.9Bn | **58%** | **23.3Bn** | 5.2Bn | **+348%** ← the engine |
| Leisure | 66.9Bn | 12% | **(7.8Bn) LOSS** | (7.1Bn) loss | worse |
| Transportation | 62.9Bn | 12% | 8.2Bn | 6.4Bn | +28% |
| Consumer Foods | 47.8Bn | 9% | 5.4Bn | 5.0Bn | +10% |
| Financial Services | 25.5Bn | 5% | 11.6Bn | 10.9Bn | +7% |
| Property | 12.1Bn | 2% | 3.2Bn | 1.2Bn | +171% |
| Others | 8.3Bn | 2% | (6.3Bn) loss | (6.6Bn) | — |
| **Group** | **528.9Bn** | | **37.7Bn** | 14.9Bn | **+153%** |

**Three structural facts:**
1. **Retail is now the group** — 58% of revenue, largest profit contributor. The FY26 explosion includes new-energy vehicle importation (post-liberalisation) — sustainability of this margin is THE forward question.
2. **Leisure is a 7.8Bn drag** — finance cost alone 11.6Bn (Cinnamon Life debt service). Operationally the segment is near breakeven before interest; the loss is a *financing-phase* artifact, not (yet) an operating failure.
3. **Parent cash engine ≠ tourism** (SenFin deck): FY26 dividends upstreamed Rs 13.85Bn (+29%), of which CCS ~5.4Bn, JKL ~4.5Bn, Union Assurance ~2.65Bn; hotels contributed <6%. Parent liquidity grew *through* the tourism downturn.
