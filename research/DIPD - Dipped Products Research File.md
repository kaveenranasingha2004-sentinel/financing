## 2. The headline numbers — Q1 FY26/27 (Group, Rs.'000, three months to 30 June 2026)

**A methodology note, carried over from the original file and reconfirmed by this interim's own footnote structure:** the DPL Premier Gloves amalgamation (effective 1 Aug 2025) is again flagged as affecting comparability — but the asterisk in this interim's own income statement sits specifically against the **Company** columns, not Group, exactly matching the original file's finding that Group economics are unaffected (DPL Premier Gloves was already consolidated as a subsidiary before the merger). **Value off Group, not Company — Company's own >100% revenue "growth" this quarter is the amalgamation artefact, not organic performance.**

| Group | Q1 FY26/27 | Q1 FY25/26 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 25,024,627 | 22,269,974 | **+12%** |
| Gross profit | 5,170,282 | 4,240,290 | **+22%** |
| Gross margin | 20.66% | 19.04% | **+1.6 pts** |
| Administrative expenses | (2,912,572) | (2,361,617) | **+23%** |
| Finance costs | (393,961) | (265,670) | **+48%** |
| Finance income | 593,864 | 284,401 | **+109%** |
| Profit before tax | 1,942,724 | 1,516,128 | +28% |
| Tax expense | (491,871) | (443,595) | +11% (effective rate 25.3% ← 29.3%) |
| Profit attributable to owners | 1,233,357 | 878,672 | **+40%** |

**Read this precisely.** Revenue and margin both genuinely improved (gross margin +1.6pt, on top of FY26's own margin gain) — this is not a low-quality print. But administrative expenses (+23%) and finance costs (+48%) both grew **faster than revenue (+12%)** — the same shape of cost-outrunning-revenue story flagged in HAYL's own file, now visible at the subsidiary level too. The **effective tax rate fell** (29.3% → 25.3%), a genuine positive reversing FY26's tax-drag story, and partly explains why PAT (+35% Group) and EPS (+40%) grew faster than PBT (+28%).

## 4. The debt-lag risk — the file's central named risk, now confirmed live

**This is the most important finding in this update.** The original file's floor derivation named one dominant risk: *"the named risk is not today's leverage ratio — it's that FY27 will carry a full year of interest on a debt base that FY26 barely felt."* Q1 FY26/27 is the first quarter to test that prediction, and it tests positive:

| Group, Rs.'000 | 30/06/2026 | 31/03/2026 | 30/06/2025 | QoQ | YoY |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LT interest-bearing loans | 4,722,884 | 4,811,958 | 2,930,427 | −1.9% | +61.2% |
| ST loans + bank overdrafts | 10,986,741 | 8,200,387 | 7,160,002 | +34.0% | +53.4% |
| ST portion of LT loans | 1,730,083 | 1,887,942 | 1,849,576 | −8.4% | −6.5% |
| **Total interest-bearing debt** | **17,439,708** | **14,900,287** | **11,940,005** | **+17.0%** | **+46.1%** |
| Total equity | 44,706,567 | 43,512,213 | 41,023,941 | +2.7% | +9.0% |
| **Debt/Equity** | **39.0%** | **34.2%** | **29.1%** | | |

**Total debt grew 17% in a single quarter** — a continuation, not a plateau, of FY26's +52.7% full-year re-leveraging. **Finance costs followed exactly as the risk predicted: +48% YoY this quarter alone** (Rs. 393,961k vs Rs. 265,670k), a materially faster acceleration than FY26's own full-year +3% (which the original file explicitly flagged as artificially subdued because most of FY26's debt was drawn late in the year). **The debt-lag risk this file was built to watch for is no longer hypothetical — it is this quarter's result.**

**A secondary observation worth flagging, not yet a named risk:** this quarter's incremental borrowing shifted toward short-term (ST loans/overdrafts +34% QoQ) while new long-term loans obtained were small (Rs. 285,562k, versus Rs. 1,697,469k in the same quarter last year) and long-term debt actually shrank slightly QoQ. If this composition shift continues, it would compound the interest-rate exposure rather than term it out — worth a second quarter's confirmation before treating it as a standing risk line, per this file's own discipline.

**Capex continues funding the debt build:** PP&E purchases Rs. 2,367,308k this quarter, nearly double the Rs. 1,208,102k spent in the same quarter last year — PP&E on the balance sheet is now Rs. 37.2 Bn (Group), up 20% YoY. **Operating cash flow remains negative but improved**: Rs. (145,457)k this quarter vs Rs. (629,911)k a year earlier — a real, if still-negative, improvement, driven mainly by receivables consuming less cash relative to the scale of the quarter's revenue growth than in the comparative period.

## 6. Valuation — hurdle method, levels held

**Hurdle:** long T-bond ≈12.6% → neutral P/E ≈ **7.9×**, unchanged.

**Sustainable EPS — still the file's stated weak point, and this quarter doesn't resolve it.** The original file's estimate (Rs. 5.80, a two-point average of adjusted FY25 and FY26 EPS) is explicitly provisional pending the annual report. Q1 FY27's annualised run-rate (2.06 × 4 ≈ 8.24) sits well above that estimate — but **one strong quarter, with no seasonality history to check it against and with finance costs simultaneously accelerating, is not grounds to revise a sustainable-earnings estimate**, per the same standing rule SINS's and HAYL's files apply to single-quarter outliers in either direction. **Sustainable EPS held at Rs. 5.80, provisional, pending the annual report.**

**Floor multiple — held at 6.2×.** The named debt-lag risk didn't get worse or better this quarter; it arrived, on schedule, at the magnitude the original derivation was already pricing for. A risk confirming itself is not the same as a risk resolving — per this file's own method, numbers move on trigger events (a risk resolved, or a sustainable-EPS shift confirmed over a full cycle), not on a single quarter that validates an assumption already in the multiple.

**Floor = 36 (unchanged). Ceiling = 45–46 (unchanged).**

## 7. Verdict

**At Rs. 58.50: ~29% above the ceiling, ~63% above the floor** — both slightly wider than the July read (~23% / ~57% at 56.50), since price moved up modestly while the floor/ceiling didn't move at all. DIPD remains priced above where this framework will pay for it, consistent with every other Hayleys-group name in this sequence.

**Verdict unchanged: WATCHLIST — no position.** The quarter is genuinely mixed in the way this file's own method is built to handle without flinching in either direction: Hand Protection's real strength and the tax-rate improvement are both credited; the confirmed acceleration in finance costs and continued Plantations contraction are both held against the multiple. Neither side moved the number.

**What would raise the ceiling:** a full annual report giving a real multi-year EPS series that confirms Q1 FY27's run-rate as sustainable rather than a strong-quarter outlier; finance-cost growth decelerating back toward FY26's pace in Q2, showing the debt has been termed out rather than compounding.
**What would lower the floor:** finance costs continuing to accelerate past +48% YoY in Q2; the shift toward short-term borrowing (§4) continuing for a second quarter; Plantations margin compressing further past this quarter's −10.2% PBT.
