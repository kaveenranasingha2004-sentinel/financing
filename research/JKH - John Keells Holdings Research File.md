**Three structural facts, one now confirmed by Q1 FY26/27 data (§F):**

## C. Why earnings still can't anchor the floor (Layer 4, Step 2)

FY26 reported EPS 0.77 contains two identifiable one-offs:
- Net divestment gains (TSHF loss −259M + Fairfirst gain +273M + Kandy Walk Inn gain +1,523M) = **+1.54bn pretax**
- Fair-value gain on investment properties = **+2.30bn pretax**, non-cash

Stripping both (≈3.84bn pretax) and carrying through the group's PAT/PBT ratios: **adjusted EPS ≈ 0.69**. Even at that number, P/E at any sane price implies an earnings yield around 4% — nowhere near the 12.6% hurdle. **Confirms the standing instruction on this file: don't buy JKH for income; buy the assets, if at all, and value it on book, not earnings.**

## F. Q1 FY26/27 Update (quarter ended 30 June 2026, reviewed 28-Jul-2026)

**Two named risks moved from "watch for" to "confirmed, live in the data" this quarter — this is the most consequential update since the file was opened.**

**1. JKCG/Retail mean-reversion — confirmed.** Retail EBITDA **−16% YoY**. Vehicles handed over actually *rose* (2,400 vs 2,300 a year ago) — this is not a demand collapse. The report states it plainly: EBITDA fell because of **"a change in mix of vehicles with a higher proportion of vehicle handovers from lower-priced segments"** (BYD ATTO 1/2 taking share) as pent-up post-liberalisation demand normalises. Volume held; margin didn't. Exactly the shape a real mean-reversion risk takes.

**2. JKCG Customs guarantee dispute — escalating, not resolving.** Group guarantee commitments: **Rs.11,686mn (31-Mar-26) → Rs.15,295mn (30-Jun-26)**, +31% in a single quarter, still tied to the unresolved "motor power" classification dispute now at the Court of Appeal.

**3. LKR depreciation hit exactly where the file said it would.** Rupee moved **315 → 337** intra-quarter (~7%). CODSL's USD-denominated term loan produced a **Rs.3.72bn net exchange loss** (vs Rs.726mn a year ago) — this is why headline profit attributable to equity holders looks almost nil (**Rs.62mn, EPS 0.004**). **Strip the FX noise and the real number is Rs.1.95bn — up from a Rs.418mn *loss* a year ago.** The underlying business improved materially; the headline print says the opposite. Worth being precise about which number is being reacted to if this comes up in conversation or in the tape.

**4. Tourism — this quarter's number closes last week's open homework.** Arrivals **−9% YoY, April–June specifically** (~406,000 visitors) — this is what the file's original "−10%" bear thread was describing (a quarterly figure), not overstated after all; it was the CBSL *H1 aggregate* (−1.8%) that told a different, blended story because Jan–Mar was still strong pre-conflict. **Live regime flag, unresolved:** forward bookings improved into August following a ceasefire, but the report itself notes **"the resumption of hostilities have reintroduced a certain level of uncertainty"** as of its 28-Jul writing date. Track this as its own binary, separate from the general Middle East risk line — it has moved twice in one quarter.

**5. Two genuine positives, not yet in the risk-adjusted floor because they don't change the named risks, only the ceiling-side picture:**
- Transportation EBITDA **+223%**, LMS bunkering volumes +38%, explicitly benefiting from Middle East supply disruption — the "war windfall" flagged as a Transportation risk was, this quarter, a tailwind. Same regime binary as tourism, opposite sign.
- NTB's integration of HSBC Sri Lanka's retail banking franchise (effective 1-May-2026) is already accretive, not just a stated plan.

**6. Balance sheet — a new watch item, not yet a named risk.** Bank overdrafts rose Rs.44.1bn → Rs.54.1bn, short-term borrowings up, while long-term interest-bearing debt fell (108.3bn → 106.6bn). A shift toward shorter-duration funding in one quarter — smaller in scale than the equivalent flags already carried on DIPD and HAYC, but the same shape, and worth watching for a second consecutive quarter before it earns a line in §D's risk list.

**Where this leaves the price:** Q1 FY26/27 range 18.20–21.00, last traded **20.20**. NAV/share confirmed **23.46**. Still inside the 17.00 floor / 23.50–24.00 ceiling band. **No falsifier has fired. HOLD stands** — but two of the five risks priced into the floor multiple are no longer hypothetical; they are this quarter's results.
