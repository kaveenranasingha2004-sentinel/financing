## 1. What the company is
Pure life insurer (Schaffter family group), Diri Savi Board, plus **25% of First Capital Holdings** (equity-accounted, carried 4.02 Bn). Ownership: Janashakthi Ltd (=JXG) **74.24%** + First Capital Limited 5.78% → group ~80%; public 19.97%. Directors hold nil shares; two new independent NEDs added Apr 2026. Borrowings modest and amortising (1.49 Bn).

## 2. The four questions — answered

**Q1 — Portfolio: an equity-heavy trading book, now being de-risked.** Investments 32.2 Bn at Mar-26: FVTPL 19.1 Bn (59%), L&R 10.8 Bn, AFS 2.3 Bn, no HTM. Inside the FVTPL book (Dec-25): govt securities 13.5 Bn, **listed equities 5.9 Bn (up 59% during 2025), unit trusts 2.6 Bn** — all held-for-trading. JINS holds equities equal to ~35% of shareholders' equity, marked through P&L. **Q1'26 action:** FVTPL cut 22.1 → 19.1 Bn while L&R grew 7.6 → 10.8 Bn — management rotated ~3 Bn into amortised-cost paper during the quarter, locking high yields and shrinking the mark-to-market surface. Rational, same direction as AAIC's duration extension.

**Q2 — Float growth: fastest of the three, and accelerating.** GWP: FY25 8.65 Bn (+31%); **Q1'26 2.60 Bn (+42%)** — first-year 1.82 Bn (+51%), renewal 0.78 Bn (+25%). Still **70% first-year** — the growth is real but the book is young and unproven.

**Q3 — Persistency: managed but not disclosed.** The AR describes a dedicated Customer Retention Unit for orphaned policies and improving persistency — but publishes **no Y1/Y2/Y3 ladder** (AAIC: 86/66/54). Narrative without numbers scores as a disclosure deficit; the first-year-heavy mix keeps this the book's biggest open risk.

**Q4 — Capital: the falsified prediction.** **CAR 284%** — *highest of the three* (AAIC 245%, UAL 215%), comfortably above the 120% floor. The provisional file's worry that borrowings signalled capital strain was wrong.

## 3. Earnings quality — how the sector thesis inverts here
FY2025: PBT 3.95 Bn, PAT 3.47 Bn, ROE 21%, EPS 5.10 post-split — **down 23% from FY2024's blowout** (PAT 4.51 Bn, EPS 6.64 post-split). Composition across 2025: FV gains 1.12 Bn + realised gains + FCH associate profit (~1.0 Bn through 9M) — i.e., roughly **half of earnings are market-linked**, not underwriting.

**Q1 2026 autopsy (PAT −61% to 113.7 Mn) — the honest decomposition, correcting my provisional hypothesis:**

Meanwhile the insurance operation itself had its best quarter in the dataset. The same macro event that feeds AAIC/UAL hits JINS twice (equity portfolio + FCH), while its core business quietly improves underneath.

## 4. The dividend pattern — JXG's cash pump
FY2025 dividend: **3,398 Mn = 98% of profit** ("historic dividend," per the AR's own narrative). 2023: 3,000 Mn — similar ratio. Equity is nearly flat across five quarters (16.90 → 16.75 Bn) despite 3.5 Bn of annual profit: everything gets paid out. **~2.52 Bn of the FY25 dividend flowed up to Janashakthi Ltd (JXG).** Compare payouts: AAIC 30%, UAL 87%, JINS 98%. JINS is operated as the holdco's cash source — fine for JXG shareholders (me, indirectly) as long as CAR stays fat (it is, at 284%), but it means JINS compounds nothing internally; growth is financed by float, not retained earnings.
