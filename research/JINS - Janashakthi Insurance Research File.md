## 1. What the company is
Pure life insurer (Schaffter family group), Diri Savi Board, plus **25% of First Capital Holdings** (equity-accounted, carried 4.02 Bn at FY25-end; **3.85 Bn at 30-Jun-2026**, down 7.0% on the associate's H1 loss). Ownership: Janashakthi Ltd (=JXG) **74.24%** + First Capital Limited 5.77% → group ~80%; public 19.97% — both confirmed unchanged in this interim's own Top 20 register. Directors hold nil shares. Borrowings modest and continuing to amortise (1.49 Bn → **1.35 Bn** at 30-Jun-2026, −16.7% YoY).

**Cross-file note, not acted on here:** JXG's own file names First Capital Holdings as a direct 58.02% look-through leg, explicitly rate-sensitive ("bond trading gains/losses"). This interim confirms the same instrument is *also* dragging JINS, which JXG owns 74.24% of. A rate-up move therefore hits JXG three ways at once — directly via FCH, indirectly via JINS's 25% FCH stake, and via JINS's own FVTPL book. Worth a line in JXG's own file next time it's opened; not edited here since this update is scoped to JINS.

## 2. The four questions — answered

**Q1 — Portfolio: an equity-heavy trading book, now being de-risked — and the de-risking has continued for two full quarters.** Investments 32.2 Bn at Mar-26 (Q1): FVTPL 19.1 Bn, L&R 10.8 Bn. **At 30-Jun-2026: FVTPL down further to 18.36 Bn (−16.8% since Dec-25, −3.8% further since Q1), L&R up to 11.15 Bn (+47.0% since Dec-25), AFS up to 2.43 Bn (+32.1%).** FVTPL's share of the total book has fallen from 70.1% (Dec-25) to 57.5% (Jun-26) — a genuine, sustained de-risking trend across two consecutive quarters, not a one-off Q1 rebalancing. **This interim doesn't disaggregate FVTPL by asset class** (govt securities vs listed equities vs unit trusts), so the ~5.9 Bn listed-equity exposure cited from the Annual Report can't be independently re-confirmed this quarter — flagged as homework, not assumed resolved.

**Q2 — Float growth: still the fastest of the three, moderating slightly but still well above the book's own prior pace.** GWP: FY25 8.65 Bn (+31%); Q1'26 2.60 Bn (+42%); **Q2'26 2.51 Bn (+29.7% YoY)**; **H1'26 5.11 Bn (+35.6% YoY)**, NWP 4.90 Bn (+35.4%). Growth decelerated from Q1's 42% to Q2's 30%, still a strong number in absolute terms — nothing here reads as the growth story breaking.

**Q3 — Persistency: still undisclosed.** No Y1/Y2/Y3 ladder in this interim either — three consecutive filings now carrying this gap. Unchanged, unconfirmed, still the book's biggest disclosure deficit.

**Q4 — Capital: still undisclosed this quarter.** No CAR figure appears anywhere in this interim (same gap pattern now showing up across the vault's insurer files generally — AAIC's H1 2026 interim had the same omission). Last confirmed reading remains **284%** from the FY2025 Annual Report, now roughly six months stale rather than fully current. Flagged as open homework, same urgency class as AAIC's and LIOC's equivalent gaps.

## 3. Earnings quality — how the sector thesis inverts here, now with a second quarter confirming it

FY2025: PBT 3.95 Bn, PAT 3.47 Bn, ROE 21%, EPS 5.10 post-split — down 23% from FY2024's blowout. Roughly half of FY2025 earnings were market-linked (FV gains + realised gains + FCH associate profit), not underwriting.

**The full H1 2026 decomposition (the exact numbers reconcile to the rupee):**

| Driver | H1 2026 swing vs H1 2025 |
|---|---|
| Realised gains (41.2 Mn vs 1,764.0 Mn) | **−1,722.8 Mn — the single largest driver, bigger than the whole PBT swing on its own** |
| First Capital Holdings associate (−288.9 Mn vs +660.0 Mn) | **−948.9 Mn** |
| Fair value marks (−334.9 Mn vs −1,315.2 Mn) | **+980.4 Mn — helped again, as in Q1** |
| Core underwriting result (NWP less benefits/claims/acquisition less admin) | **+627.5 Mn — improved, for the second consecutive half** |
| Investment income (recurring) | −210.2 Mn |
| Finance cost, fee income, other operating revenue (net) | +36.7 Mn |
| **= Total PBT swing** | **−1,237.4 Mn (exact reconciliation to the reported 271.5 Mn vs 1,508.9 Mn)** |

**Quarter-by-quarter, the pattern is consistent, not a one-off:** Q1'26 core underwriting improved ~144–220 Mn (methodology-dependent proxy) while FCH swung to a 120.4 Mn loss (from +127.9 Mn); **Q2'26 core underwriting improved a further ~483 Mn while FCH's loss widened to 168.5 Mn (from +532.1 Mn a year earlier)**. The insurance operation's underlying improvement didn't fade in Q2 — if anything it strengthened — while the associate's drag got *worse*, consistent with the vault's own hurdle rate continuing to rise through September (11.96%→12.17%) rather than easing. First Capital is a primary dealer; its trading book keeps bleeding as long as yields keep climbing.

**A genuinely useful new read this quarter: normalised recurring profitability (stripping realised gains, FV marks, and the FCH associate line entirely) more than doubled.** H1 2026 ≈ 854.1 Mn vs H1 2025 ≈ 400.1 Mn, **+113%**. This isn't a disclosed company sub-total — it's a reconstruction excluding every market-linked line this file already treats as non-recurring — but it's a cleaner, more complete version of the same "haircut the market-linked half, credit the improving core" logic the original sustainable-EPS estimate (§7) was already built on. **Read as corroborating evidence for holding that estimate, not grounds to revise it upward**: it's two quarters of data against a structural trigger (SLFRS 17 restatement) that still hasn't fired, and a method this file hasn't used before needs a second, independent confirmation before it gets to move a number.

## 4. The dividend pattern — a break worth flagging plainly

FY2025 dividend: 3,398 Mn = 98% of profit, described in the Annual Report's own words as "historic." **The Statement of Changes in Equity for H1 2026 shows "Dividend Paid to equity holders" as a flat zero** — no interim dividend declared or paid in the first half of 2026. Equity moved from 16,644 Mn to 16,771 Mn (+0.76%), almost entirely explained by retaining the period's own thin 129.6 Mn profit rather than any large capital movement. **This is new, not previously flagged**: the original file characterized the 98% payout as a settled operating characteristic ("fine for JXG shareholders as long as CAR stays fat"). With profit near zero this half, there was little to pay out regardless — but the practical effect is that **JXG received no cash upstream from JINS in H1 2026**, worth a cross-check against JXG's own holdco debt-service picture (that file's 9M FY26 standalone numbers relied on ~3,967 Mn of subsidiary dividends against ~1,656 Mn of standalone finance cost — a cushion this pause narrows, at least for this half). Not acted on in JXG's file here; flagged for when that file is next opened.
