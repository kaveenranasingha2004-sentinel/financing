## A. The Business

Healthcare-led conglomerate. Five divisions, three reportable segments:

| Segment | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Healthcare** | Manufacturing, importing, marketing pharmaceuticals, nutraceuticals, medical diagnostics, surgical products |
| **Agribusiness** | Crude **palm oil** cultivation + dairy farming (⚠ NOT tea — common misconception) |
| **Consumer goods** | Value-added tea export, confectionery, organic spices |
| Other | Management services, rental (below reporting threshold) |

### Segment split — FY2025/26 (external revenue LKR 65.9Bn)

| Segment | Revenue | % Rev | PBT | % of segment PBT | PBT margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Healthcare | 37.4Bn | **56.8%** | 4.64Bn | 49.5% | 12.4% |
| Consumer goods | 19.0Bn | 28.9% | 1.10Bn | 11.8% | 5.8% |
| Agribusiness | 9.4Bn | 14.3% | 3.63Bn | **38.7%** | **38.4%** |

**The defining tension:** SUN is a healthcare company by revenue, but agribusiness (palm oil) is the disproportionate profit engine — 14% of revenue delivering 39% of profit at triple the group margin.

### YoY segment PBT movement

| Segment | FY25 | FY26 | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Healthcare | 5.33Bn | 4.64Bn | **−13%** ← margin compression despite +15% revenue |
| Agribusiness | 2.90Bn | 3.63Bn | **+25%** ← drove group growth |
| Consumer | 0.90Bn | 1.10Bn | +23% |

Open question (unanswered): *why did healthcare margins compress?* Candidates: pharma price controls, LKR import-cost inflation, JV startup costs. → Check MD&A / next interims.
