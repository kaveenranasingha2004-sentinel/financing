## A. The Business

Healthcare-led conglomerate. Five divisions, three reportable segments:

| Segment | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Healthcare** | Manufacturing, importing, marketing pharmaceuticals, nutraceuticals, medical diagnostics, surgical products |
| **Agribusiness** | Crude **palm oil** cultivation + dairy farming |
| **Consumer goods** | Value-added tea export, confectionery, organic spices — **now including JAPC (75%-effective spice/coconut/tea export acquisition), consolidated for the first time in Q1 FY27** |

### Segment split — FY2025/26 (external revenue LKR 65.9Bn)

| Segment | Revenue | % Rev | PBT | % of segment PBT | PBT margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Healthcare | 37.4Bn | 56.8% | 4.64Bn | 49.5% | 12.4% |

**The defining tension:** SUN is a healthcare company by revenue, but agribusiness (palm oil) is the disproportionate profit engine — 14% of revenue delivering 39% of profit at triple the group margin.

## A1. Q1 FY2026/27 Update (quarter ended 30 June 2026, reviewed 08-Aug-2026)

### The headline numbers (Group, Rs.'000, three months to 30/06/2026)

| | Q1 FY27 | Q1 FY26 (restated) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 18,537,511 | 15,893,262 | +17% |
| Gross profit | 5,215,406 | 5,053,037 | +3% |
| Gross margin | 28.1% | 31.8% | −366 bps |
| Results from operating activities (EBIT) | 2,440,140 | 2,644,593 | −8% |
| Net finance income | 159,693 | 11,092 | +1,340% (see caveat below) |
| Profit before tax | 2,599,834 | 2,655,685 | −2% |
| Profit for the period (Group) | 1,407,206 | 1,327,469 | +6% |
| — attributable to owners | 665,366 | 674,738 | **−1%** |
| — attributable to NCI | 741,840 | 652,731 | +14% |
| **Basic EPS (Rs.)** | **0.34** | 0.41 | **−17%** |

**Read:** Group PAT looks fine (+6%), but that headline hides the number that actually matters to you as a shareholder — owners' profit fell, and EPS fell 17% YoY on a clean, same-quarter comparison. The gap is NCI: minorities took **52.7% of this quarter's group profit**, up from ~40% for all of FY26. JAPC's 25% minority stake is now flowing through, compounding an already-real leakage problem.

**The net finance income swing (+1,340%) needs a caveat.** At Company (standalone) level, finance income was flat-to-down (25,957 vs 29,351). The entire swing is a Group-level phenomenon, plausibly linked to cash balances roughly doubling (6,545mn vs 3,580mn a year earlier) — but a 172% jump in finance income against an ~83% jump in cash isn't fully explained by that alone. **Open question:** what else is inside that line. Worth checking the FY26 Annual Report's finance-income note next time it's open.

### Segment picture

| Segment | Q1 FY27 Revenue | Q1 FY26 Revenue | Rev Δ | Q1 FY27 Op. Profit | Q1 FY26 Op. Profit | Profit Δ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Healthcare | 9,128,944 | 8,619,475 | +5.9% | 789,797 | 1,210,475 | **−34.8%** |
| Consumer | 6,597,257 | 4,781,461 | +38.0% (+2.1% ex-JAPC) | 368,106 | 268,515 | +37.1% |
| Agribusiness | 2,809,495 | 2,491,501 | +12.8% | 1,164,805 | 1,216,202 | −4.2% |

**Healthcare — confirmed as the central risk, and accelerating, not stabilizing.** The GCEO's letter names the cause directly: **NMRA-driven price reductions** — the "pharma price controls" candidate from the original file's open question, now confirmed. Pharmaceutical agency business contracted **18.2%** on lower volumes and "industry-wide constraints." Medical devices +8.8%. Healthguard Distribution −0.3%, Healthguard Pharmacy −0.9%. Lina +20.5%, "supported by government product deliveries" — a tender/government-dependent revenue stream, a concentration risk not previously named. **A 35% operating-profit decline on a segment that's 49% of group revenue is worse than the FY26 annual figure's −13% PBT decline.**

**Consumer — genuinely accretive acquisition, but headline growth is misleading if read naively.** Ex-JAPC organic growth is only 2.1% — branded tea +8.3% (Watawala Thei, Ran Kahata performing; **Zesta facing intensified premium-segment competition**, a new named risk), confectionery +6.9%, tea exports −6.3% on lower volumes from key clients (customer concentration, also newly named). JAPC: spice exports +11.5% (cinnamon, coconut, black pepper, clove). Segment operating profit grew roughly in line with revenue (+37% vs +38%) — JAPC is not diluting margins, a genuine execution positive.

**Agribusiness — the El Niño falsifier has not fired, and this quarter argues the opposite.** Oil palm revenue +17.9% "supported by higher prices and volumes, underpinned by **improved crop availability**." No weather damage visible yet. Dairy declined −7.1% on lower milk volumes and higher input costs — a smaller, different risk materializing instead. Agribusiness EBIT margin moderated slightly (48.8% → 47.2%) but remains the group's best margin by far. Segment operating profit fell 4.2% despite revenue growth — GCEO letter attributes this to "higher operating costs in Agribusiness," a cost-side story, not demand-side.

### New, mild balance-sheet flag

Current borrowings +25% (6,421.8mn → 8,026.1mn) and bank overdraft +42% (1,328.0mn → 1,889.6mn) in a single quarter, funding inventory (+12.6%) and receivables (+9.5%) growth alongside JAPC integration. Not alarming in isolation, but the same shape of short-term-debt-funds-working-capital-growth pattern flagged as an early-warning sign on DIPD and HAYC — worth watching for a second consecutive quarter before it earns a named risk line in the floor.

## C. Valuation — hurdle method (re-derived; supersedes the original non-hurdle-anchored P/E bands)

**Hurdle:** long T-bond ≈12.6% → neutral P/E ≈ **7.9×**.

**Sustainable EPS:** 4-year average (FY23–FY26: 1.14, 1.94, 1.77, 1.55) ≈ **Rs. 1.60** — close to FY26's own 1.55, passing the same consistency check HAYL's window used. No one-off contamination identified in any year. **Caveat:** Q1 FY27's −17% YoY quarterly EPS is a live risk this average proves too generous once FY27 closes — flagged, not yet adjusted for.

**Floor multiple — cut for six named risks:**
1. Healthcare margin compression — confirmed structural (NMRA pricing), accelerating (−34.8% segment operating profit this quarter), hitting the largest segment (49% of revenue).
2. NCI leakage rising — now >50% of quarterly profit to minorities.
3. 116% FY26 payout ratio — unsustainable without an EPS recovery that isn't currently happening.
4. Two consecutive annual EPS declines, plus a quarter's worth of live evidence (−17% YoY) that a third year is plausible.
5. Newly named concentration risk — tea-export key clients, Zesta premium competition, Lina's government-tender dependence.
6. El Niño — still live, unconfirmed, sits directly over the profit engine (palm oil/dairy).

**Floor multiple: 4.5×** — below ALUM's 5.0×, in the same range as SINS's 6-risk 4.8× but without SINS's offsetting positives (best-in-queue interest cover, external credit rating) to pull it back up.

**Floor** = 1.60 × 4.5 ≈ 7.2, rounded down → **Rs. 7.00**
**Second tranche** = Floor × 0.90 → **Rs. 6.30**
**Ceiling** = 1.60 × 7.9 (hurdle-neutral, no growth stretch — the core segment is shrinking) ≈ 12.64, rounded down → **Rs. 12.50**
