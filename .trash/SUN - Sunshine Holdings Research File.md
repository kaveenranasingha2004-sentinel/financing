## A1. Q1 FY2026/27 Update (quarter ended 30 June 2026)

| | Q1 FY27 | Q1 FY26 (restated) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 18,537,511 | 15,893,262 | +17% |
| Gross margin | 28.1% | 31.8% | −366 bps |
| EBIT | 2,440,140 | 2,644,593 | −8% |
| Profit for the period (Group) | 1,407,206 | 1,327,469 | +6% |
| — attributable to owners | 665,366 | 674,738 | −1% |
| — attributable to NCI | 741,840 | 652,731 | +14% |
| **Basic EPS (Rs.)** | **0.34** | 0.41 | **−17%** |

**Healthcare confirmed as the central risk, and it's accelerating.** GCEO's letter names NMRA-driven price reductions directly. Segment operating profit −34.8% YoY (789.8mn vs 1,210.5mn) on only +5.9% revenue — worse than the FY26 annual −13% PBT decline. Pharmaceutical agency business −18.2%.

**NCI leakage worsened**: 52.7% of quarterly profit to minorities, up from ~40% for all of FY26 — JAPC's 25% minority stake compounding an existing problem.

**Consumer**: ex-JAPC organic growth only +2.1%. JAPC itself accretive at the operating-profit level (+37% profit vs +38% revenue). New named risks: Zesta facing intensified premium-tea competition; tea-export volume concentration in a few key clients; Lina's growth driven by government-tender deliveries.

**Agribusiness**: oil palm +17.9% on improved crop availability — **El Niño falsifier not firing this quarter.** Dairy −7.1% on lower volumes/higher input costs. Segment operating profit still fell 4.2% despite revenue growth, on higher operating costs.

**Zydus JV formally established** (26-Jun-2026) — bull thread advanced from announced to executed, USD 20mn committed.

**New, mild balance-sheet flag**: current borrowings +25%, bank overdraft +42%, funding inventory/receivables growth alongside JAPC integration — the same early-warning shape flagged on DIPD/HAYC, one quarter old, not yet a named risk in the floor.

## C. Valuation — hurdle method (re-derived; supersedes the original non-hurdle-anchored P/E bands)

**Hurdle:** long T-bond ≈12.6% → neutral P/E ≈ **7.9×**.

**Sustainable EPS:** 4-year average (FY23–FY26: 1.14, 1.94, 1.77, 1.55) ≈ **Rs. 1.60** — close to FY26's own 1.55, passing the same consistency check HAYL's window used. No one-off contamination identified in any year. **Caveat:** Q1 FY27's −17% YoY quarterly EPS is a live risk this average proves too generous once FY27 closes — flagged, not yet adjusted for.

**Floor multiple — six named risks:**
1. Healthcare margin compression — confirmed structural (NMRA pricing), accelerating, hitting 49% of group revenue.
2. NCI leakage — now >50% of quarterly profit.
3. 116% FY26 payout ratio — unsustainable without EPS recovery.
4. Two annual EPS declines plus live Q1 FY27 evidence of a third.
5. Named concentration risk — tea-export key clients, Zesta competition, Lina's government-tender dependence.
6. El Niño — live, unconfirmed, sits over the profit engine.

**Floor multiple: 4.5×**

**Floor** = 1.60 × 4.5 ≈ 7.2, rounded down → **Rs. 7.00**
**Second tranche** = Floor × 0.90 → **Rs. 6.30**
**Ceiling** = 1.60 × 7.9 (hurdle-neutral, no growth stretch — the core segment is shrinking) ≈ 12.64, rounded down → **Rs. 12.50**
